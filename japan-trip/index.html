Paid attractions worth considering

Yes, there are several good ones. But for this 2.5-day trip, I would add only one if you want more paid sightseeing.

BEST FIT

Sumida Aquarium

It is in Tokyo Skytree Town, so it adds almost no transport overhead. It has penguins, fur seals and a large jellyfish display. General admission is currently listed at ¥2,500.

My recommendation: Add this only if you are willing to shorten Kappabashi or Skytree time. It is the easiest extra paid attraction operationally.

Current detailsMap
LOW-COST CULTURE

Edo-Tokyo Museum

The museum reopened on March 31, 2026 after renovation. The permanent exhibition is ¥800 for adults. It is excellent for understanding Edo and Tokyo, but it is not on the current route and would replace Ueno/Akihabara/Kappabashi time.

My recommendation: Skip on this trip unless history is a major priority.

Official ticketsMap
HIGHER-COST ALTERNATIVE

teamLab Borderless

Very good immersive digital art, with adult admission currently starting at ¥3,600 and dynamic pricing. It is in Azabudai Hills, far from the current east-Tokyo route.

My recommendation: Only choose it by replacing Akihabara + Kappabashi. Do not try to squeeze it into the existing schedule.

Official tickets/detailsMap
SKIP THIS TRIP

Tokyo Tower / SHIBUYA SKY

Both are good paid viewpoints. Tokyo Tower Main Deck is ¥1,500 and SHIBUYA SKY online tickets are ¥2,700 before 15:00 or ¥3,400 from 15:00. But you already have Tokyo Skytree, and these require more cross-city travel.

My recommendation: Do not add another observation deck. Use that time for the neighborhoods already in the plan.

Tokyo Tower pricesSHIBUYA SKY details

If you really want a second paid experience, Sumida Aquarium is the only one I would add because it is next to Skytree and requires almost no extra travel. teamLab Borderless is better saved for a longer Tokyo trip.

Train playbook

Never chase a specific train. Chase the correct line, direction and destination.

Narita T2 → Honjo-azumabashi

Preferred: Keisei Access Express / through service to Toei Asakusa Line. Target about 1 hour to the hotel area. Exact Honjo fare varies by route and should be read at the gate/IC fare.

Fallback: next suitable through service. If needed, take a service to Oshiage/Asakusa and follow station staff instructions rather than improvising a long transfer.
