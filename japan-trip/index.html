Paid attractions worth considering

For this short trip, add only if you are happy to replace something else.

BEST ADD-ON

Sumida Aquarium

Inside Tokyo Skytree Town. Penguins, fur seals and jellyfish. Current general admission planning figure: ¥2,500.

Recommendation: easiest second paid attraction because it adds almost no transport time.

LOW-COST CULTURE

Edo-Tokyo Museum

Reopened March 31, 2026. Permanent exhibition adult admission is ¥800. Great history option, but it would replace part of the Ueno/Akihabara/Kappabashi route.

Official details
IMMERSIVE ART

teamLab Borderless

Adult admission currently starts around ¥3,600 with dynamic pricing. Excellent, but far from the east-Tokyo route.

Recommendation: only choose it by replacing Akihabara + Kappabashi.

Official details
SKIP

Tokyo Tower / SHIBUYA SKY

Both are good viewpoints, but you already have Skytree. Do not spend your limited time crossing Tokyo for another observation deck.
